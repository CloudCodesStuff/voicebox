# Agency outreach — manual, no tools

The play: agencies and studios install Voicebox on every client site they run.
One closed agency = 5–20 installs and a retainer line item for them. Everything
below is hand-done. No Explee, no automation on LinkedIn (that's how accounts
get restricted).

The unfair advantage is the demo: the widget brand-matches any site
automatically. So every first message is a screenshot of the widget already
sitting on one of *their* client sites, not a pitch.

---

## 1. Fix your LinkedIn profile first (10 minutes, once)

People click your name before they reply. The profile is the landing page.

**Headline**

> Building Voicebox — feedback widgets for client sites. Installs in 4
> minutes, matches the brand on its own.

**About**

> Agencies hand off a site and never hear what users actually struggle with.
> Voicebox is a feedback widget that installs with one script tag, matches the
> client's brand automatically, and turns every reply into a ranked list of
> what to fix. One dashboard, a project per client. I'm the founder; DM me and
> you'll get me, not a sequence.

**Featured section** (in this order)

1. Link: usevoicebox.dev/agencies
2. A 30-second screen recording: paste tag → widget appears matched → themes
   dashboard. (Record once with QuickTime, reuse everywhere.)
3. Link: usevoicebox.dev/pricing

---

## 2. Build the list (15 prospects/day, no more)

Where to find agencies whose stack you already support:

- Webflow Experts directory (experts.webflow.com) — filter by small studios
- Framer Experts / Partners directory
- Shopify Partners directory (agencies, not app devs)
- "Site by [studio]" credits in footers of sites you like
- Dribbble / Contra studio profiles with live client work linked

Qualify in 30 seconds: do they show **3+ live client sites** in a portfolio,
and are they active (posted or shipped something in the last 2 months)? If
yes, they're on the list. Find the founder or a partner, not a designer.

Track in one sheet: `studio | person | client site used for demo | invited |
accepted | msg 1 | reply | call`.

---

## 3. Connection request (no pitch, ever)

Blank is fine. If you write anything, one line about their work:

> Saw the [client] site you shipped — clean work. Connecting.

Pitching in the invite is why acceptance rates die. 15/day max; LinkedIn
restricts accounts that push volume, especially newer ones.

---

## 4. The demo move (do this BEFORE messaging)

For each prospect who accepts:

1. In Voicebox: new project, enter one of their client sites' URLs.
2. The widget studio preview auto-matches that site's colors, corners, font.
3. Screenshot the preview sitting on their client's brand.
4. Delete the project after (or keep a "demos" project and re-match).

Two minutes per prospect. This screenshot is the entire pitch.

---

## 5. Messages

**Message 1** (after accept, with the screenshot attached)

> Ran our widget against [client site] you built — it matched their brand on
> its own, zero config. That's what their users would see.
>
> It groups everything users submit into a ranked fix list, one project per
> client, your branding not ours. Agencies put it in the retainer.
>
> Worth 15 minutes?

**Follow-up** (3–4 days later, only if silence)

> One more angle: the monthly "what should we fix" client call. The ranked
> theme list is that agenda, written by their users. Happy to set up your
> first client free so you can see it with real traffic.

**Breakup** (7 days after that)

> Closing the loop — if feedback reporting ever becomes part of your
> retainers, usevoicebox.dev/agencies has the details. Good luck with
> [whatever they're currently shipping].

Then stop. No fourth message.

**If they reply with interest:** offer to install it on ONE client site
together on a 15-minute call. Free plan, their hands on the keyboard. The
product closes from there.

---

## 6. Cold email variant (for prospects with a public email, no LinkedIn)

Manual, one at a time, from your own address. Keep it 1:1 and personal —
you're a founder writing a note, not running a campaign. Stop immediately if
someone asks. Do not bulk-send; bulk commercial email has legal requirements
(address, unsubscribe) we deliberately don't meet.

Subject: `[client site] + a widget that matched it on its own`

Body = Message 1 with the screenshot inline, plus a one-line sign-off with
your name.

---

## 7. The daily 30 minutes

- 10 min: add 15 new prospects to the sheet, send 15 invites
- 10 min: run the demo move + Message 1 for everyone who accepted
- 5 min: follow-ups due today (check the sheet)
- 5 min: reply to anything that came back, book calls

Expectations so you don't quit in week one: ~30–40% of invites accept, a
screenshot-first message gets replies a pitch never will, but this is still
outreach — 15/day for two weeks is ~150 invites, ~50 conversations started,
and a handful of real calls. One closed agency pays for the month.
