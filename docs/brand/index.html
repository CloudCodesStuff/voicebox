01 — Logo

Wordmark & mark

The mark is a progress line hitting its final node — the moment a job goes Ready. Space Grotesk, tight tracking, orange period. The period is the brand: the job is done. Full stop.

02 — Color

Palette

One signal color, used sparingly. Orange means "in motion." Green is reserved for exactly one thing: Ready. Everything else stays quiet so those two always read at a glance — including in sunlight on a greasy phone.

Why it matters

Even the customer who won't give an email leaves holding the status link. Checking the QR replaces making the call — that's the no-SMS gap, closed on paper.

07 — Voice

How Readyline talks

Shop-floor plain. Short sentences. Say what happened and what happens next. No exclamation points, no "we're thrilled," no emoji — the customer-facing calm is the premium.

✓ Yes

Your brakes are done. Ready for pickup any time before 6.

We found a cracked rotor. Fixing it is $180 on top of the quote. Approve below and we'll keep going.

Still waiting on a part. New estimate: Thursday.

✗ No

Great news!! 🎉 Your vehicle service journey has reached an exciting milestone!

We've identified an additional service opportunity that requires your authorization at your earliest convenience.

Thank you for your patience as we continue to strive to deliver excellence.
